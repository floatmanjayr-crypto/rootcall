    Avoid a forwarding loop.

    Do not forward the same phone number that RootCall sends approved calls back to. That can repeatedly send calls through screening instead of reaching you.

    1. Arrange a separate, unforwarded destination for approved calls. Update your forwarding destination in dashboard Settings first.
    2. Ask your carrier for the correct forwarding setup, eligibility, charges, and how to turn it off. RootCall cannot change your carrier settings automatically.
    3. Only after confirming a loop-free destination, forward your existing number to your Shield number. Test from another phone; disable carrier forwarding if calls loop or fail.
