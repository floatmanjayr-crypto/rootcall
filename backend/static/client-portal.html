Scam patterns to watch

  • Government impersonation

    Be wary of callers demanding immediate payment or threatening legal action while claiming to represent a government agency.

  • Tech support fraud

    Unexpected calls about infected devices are often a pretext to request remote access or payment.

  • Identity threats

    Legitimate agencies will not demand gift cards or cryptocurrency to resolve an identity or account issue.
